Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Heidi Workspace Access is a private tool used only by its owner, to access the owner's own Google account.

What it accesses

With the owner's explicit consent, it requests access to the owner's Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts, in order to search, read, summarise and draft items on the owner's behalf.

How data is used and stored

  • Google account data is processed on the owner's own computer.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on that computer and are not shared with anyone.
  • Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with third parties.
  • Content may be sent to an AI model provider chosen by the owner solely to carry out the owner's own requests.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
